    Field guide

    1. Feed

      Drift into anything smaller than you. Your membrane wraps it and it dissolves into your cytoplasm. You must be about a third larger than what you swallow.

    2. Colour

      Every region of the culture has its own hue. Food close to your colour gives up to three times the mass. Eating other hues dilutes you.

    3. Digest

      Undigested food weighs on you: a full cell crawls. Bigger cells digest faster, so pace your meals.

    4. Levels and attacks

      Grow to level up: a needle injects new DNA and upgrades your attack from spit to trichocyst volley, toxicyst dart, osmotic pulse and lysosome bomb. Every attack costs mass, splits rivals and wounds bosses. Eat diatoms, rotifers, Stentor and tardigrades for a shield.

    5. Boost

      Eating small creatures charges up to two boosts. Shift or the Boost button blasts dye behind you and launches you forward.

    6. Divide and fuse

      Split to launch half of yourself at prey. Your pieces can rejoin after ten seconds. Press into a smaller rival and your membranes fuse.

    7. Dangers

      • Spiked virus. Bursts you into pieces if you are bigger than it.
      • Bdellovibrio. A red hunter that burrows in and eats you from inside.
      • Bacteriophage. Lands, injects its DNA and changes you: faster, growing, sluggish or leaking.
      • Sand, fibres, air bubbles. Solid. Flow around them.

    8. Controls

      Pointer steers · Space splits · W attacks · Shift boosts · M mutes · N music · scroll zooms.

      Left thumb steers · buttons split, attack and boost · pinch zooms.
